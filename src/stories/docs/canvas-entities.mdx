# Canvas entities: planned

**This page is a placeholder.** There are no canvas stories yet, and that is deliberate.

A gallery of what the canvas draws belongs here. It would include joints, links, slots, slides,
cylinders, forces, the glyph primitives they are composed from, and the ambiguous hit targets where
two of them overlap.

Stages 1 and 2 of `docs/joint-type-and-cylinder-plan.md` change how a slider and a cylinder are
drawn: a slider becomes one joint with one mark, and a cylinder's slide becomes a joint of its own.
Building this gallery now, against today's `Joint` and `Link` classes, would mean building it
twice.

## Where the stories will go

`src/stories/canvas/`. That directory already exists, and it holds only a `README.md` that says
the same as this page.

## Until then

- The visual grammar these stories will illustrate is written down in
  `docs/joint-types-plan.md`, §2.8 "Visual grammar". In short: link colors carry no meaning,
  carrier and rider are told apart by structure, and the driven arrow is always white.
- `docs/ui-style-guide.md` summarizes that grammar under "Canvas grammar".
