PMKSConversion used v = ω × r: start with the known input, use shared-center velocities to determine other body rates, then find point velocities. This preview retains that logic, solving the shared-center constraints together to avoid fragile divisions by one coordinate.

For a finite ground IC, vₓ = −ω(y − yᵢ), vᵧ = ω(x − xᵢ), and speed = |ω| × distance to the IC. A center at infinity represents translation.

IC velocity analysis is valid for planar rigid-body motion when the centers and rates can be determined. Nearly coincident construction lines can be ill-conditioned; unresolved or singular cases are shown as unavailable.

A zero-velocity center need not have zero acceleration. Treating a moving IC as a fixed pivot gives an incorrect acceleration formula. This preview therefore calculates velocities; positions and accelerations continue to come from the current method.

Canvas centers follow the displayed pose. The calculations use the recorded sample identified above, so the two can differ slightly during animation. Hiding centers changes only the display.

Crosses mark fixed or permanent centers; diamonds mark secondary centers. Centers at infinity and unresolved centers are listed here. Distant centers may lie outside the canvas view.
